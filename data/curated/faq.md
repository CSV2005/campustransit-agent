# CampusTransit FAQ — 40 questions students actually ask

## Passes and prices

### 1. How much does a BMTC student bus pass cost in 2026-27?
Nothing, for non-AC services. Under the state free student bus pass scheme effective
12 June 2026, the government pays the pass fee. Applying online on Seva Sindhu is
free; a Bangalore One counter may charge a Rs 30 service fee for data entry.

### 2. Is the student pass really free, or is it a discount?
It is free for non-AC BMTC and KSRTC services. The government also pays a Rs 100
processing fee per pass and Rs 50 towards the accident relief fund. AC services are
not covered, so an AC student pass still costs money.

### 3. What did the student pass cost before the free scheme?
For 2025-26: primary Rs 150, high-school boys Rs 750, high-school girls Rs 550, PUC
Rs 1,050, degree and professional Rs 1,300, technical and medical Rs 1,830, and
SC/ST students Rs 150.

### 4. How much is the BMTC AC (Vajra) student pass?
Rs 1,400 per month, following the January 2025 revision. It covers BMTC AC services
but not the Vayu Vajra airport buses.

### 5. How much is a normal BMTC monthly pass for a non-student?
Rs 1,200 per calendar month for ordinary non-AC services, revised from Rs 1,050 in
January 2025. There is a Rs 1,500 extension pass for newly extended routes beyond
40 km.

### 6. What does the Vayu Vajra Gold pass cost?
Rs 4,000 per month. It is the only pass that covers airport services, and it
includes toll and GST components.

### 7. Is there a Namma Metro student pass?
No student pass appears in BMRCL's published fare products. Metro fares are
distance-based, roughly Rs 10 to Rs 90 per journey, and the state free student pass
scheme does not cover the metro.

### 8. Do I need a pass if I am a woman?
No, if you are a Karnataka resident travelling on non-AC services. The Shakti scheme
gives free travel on BMTC ordinary and KSRTC ordinary or express buses inside
Karnataka. Carry an ID. You would only need a pass for AC or metro travel.

### 9. Does Shakti work on AC buses?
No. Shakti is limited to non-premium, non-AC services. Vajra, Vayu Vajra, Airavat,
Ambari and Rajahamsa are excluded.

### 10. Does the free student pass cover AC buses?
No. It covers non-AC services of BMTC, KSRTC, NWKRTC and KKRTC only.

### 11. I already paid for my 2026-27 pass. Can I get a refund?
Yes. Claim it on Seva Sindhu with Aadhaar e-KYC and bank details. KSRTC has said
refunds are credited within seven days. Keep using the old pass until the refund
arrives.

### 12. Is there a daily or weekly option?
Yes. BMTC ordinary daily pass Rs 80 and weekly pass Rs 350, both revised in January
2025. A Vajra AC daily pass is Rs 140.

## Documents and applications

### 13. What documents do I need for a student pass?
An institution-signed application form, the current-year fee receipt or bonafide
certificate, Aadhaar, a passport-size photograph, and a caste certificate with RD
number if you are SC/ST. A live photo is taken at the counter.

### 14. Do I have to apply, or is the pass automatic?
You must apply. The free pass is not issued automatically to anyone.

### 15. Where do I apply?
On Seva Sindhu (sevaone.karnataka.gov.in). You can also apply at Bangalore One,
Karnataka One or Grama One centres for a Rs 30 service charge.

### 16. Can my parent collect the pass for me?
No. The student must collect the smart card in person because a live photograph is
captured and printed on the card.

### 17. How long is the pass valid?
The academic year. School and PUC passes run roughly June to March; degree and
professional courses July to June; technical and medical courses August to July. ITI
passes run 12 months.

### 18. What if my college is more than 40 km from home?
You may be asked for a declaration that the course is not available at a closer
institution. KSRTC's distance norm is up to 60 km one-way. Students on the
Kanakapura Road corridor often fall in this band.

### 19. My application was rejected because the institution was not in the list. What now?
The institution's DISE or AISHE code is missing. Get the exact code from the college
office, or apply through a Bangalore One counter where the operator can enter it
manually.

### 20. My Aadhaar OTP is not arriving. What should I do?
The mobile number linked to Aadhaar is probably stale. Update it at an Aadhaar
centre and retry. Do not create a second Seva Sindhu account.

### 21. Can I apply for a pass mid-year?
Yes. The pass is issued for the remaining part of the academic year. Applying in
October is late but allowed.

### 22. What do I do if I lose my pass card?
Apply for a replacement at the issuing counter. BMTC charges a fixed replacement
fee, historically Rs 100, and the same fee applies to a route change.

## Routes and travel

### 23. Which bus goes from Jayanagar to DSU Harohalli?
Route 213-V runs Harohalli to Sanjay Gandhi Hospital through Jayanagar, so it is a
one-seat ride for much of the corridor. Route 213-VD connects Dayananda Sagara MDC
with Banashankari and Anjanapura. Both are ordinary non-AC services.

### 24. How do I get to DSU Harohalli from Majestic?
Take a Banashankari-bound bus or the Green Line metro to Banashankari, then a 213-V
or 213-VD toward Harohalli. Ask for the Dayananda Sagara MDC stop, which is at the
campus gate.

### 25. Which stop is closest to the DSU Harohalli campus?
Dayananda Sagara MDC (BMTC stop code HRHD) at about 12.663 N, 77.450 E is the campus
stop. BWSSB Quaters Harohalli (BWSSBQ) is a short walk away on the main road.

### 26. Is there a direct bus from Banashankari to Harohalli?
Yes. Route 213-VD runs between Dayananda Sagara MDC and Banashankari Bus Station,
about 59 stops, roughly 38 km.

### 27. How long is the Jayanagar to Harohalli bus ride?
About 90 minutes to two hours in peak traffic for roughly 38 km. The distance is not
the problem on this corridor; the traffic is.

### 28. Does the metro go to Harohalli?
No. The Green Line ends at Silk Institute, about 12 km north of Harohalli. From
there you need a bus.

### 29. Which metro station is closest to Harohalli?
Silk Institute on the Green Line, at 12.8616 N, 77.5299 E.

### 30. What is the KSRTC fare from Bengaluru to Kanakapura?
Rs 124 for an ordinary bus, revised on 5 January 2025 from Rs 107.

### 31. What is the KSRTC fare to Harohalli?
There is no published fare for the Harohalli stage. Scaling the Kanakapura fare by
distance suggests roughly Rs 80-90, but treat that as an estimate and confirm at the
depot or on the KSRTC app.

### 32. Can I use my BMTC student pass on a KSRTC bus?
Not unless the pass is a KSRTC pass. BMTC and KSRTC are separate corporations. Since
2026-27 the state free student pass covers non-AC services of both, but the pass
must be issued for the operator and route you use.

### 33. Do KSRTC monthly season tickets cover express buses?
A pass issued for Express services is valid on Express and Ordinary services. A pass
issued for Ordinary services is valid only on Ordinary services.

## Costs and comparisons

### 34. Is a monthly pass worth it if I spend Rs 60 a day on buses?
At Rs 60 a day for 24 days you spend Rs 1,440 a month. The ordinary monthly pass is
Rs 1,200, so you save Rs 240. If you are a student, the free pass makes it a
no-brainer: you save the full Rs 1,440.

### 35. Is the AC pass worth Rs 1,400 for a student?
Only if you actually use AC buses. Rs 1,400 is about 47 ordinary rides at Rs 30, so
you need roughly 24 AC journeys a month to break even. On route 213-V, which is
ordinary, the AC pass would be wasted money.

### 36. How much does a typical student commute cost per month?
A 38 km one-way trip at the ordinary slab is about Rs 30. Return trips for 24 days
come to about Rs 1,440. Under the free student pass, that becomes Rs 0.

### 37. Are tolls included in passes?
No. BMTC passes exclude toll charges on roads such as NICE Road; you pay the toll
separately.

### 38. Do senior citizens get a discount?
Yes. BMTC senior citizen fares are concessional, roughly Rs 5 for the first 2 km and
Rs 24 at 50 km. A senior citizen ordinary monthly pass also exists.

## Data and reliability

### 39. How current is the route and fare information?
Stop and route data comes from the BMTC and BMRCL GTFS feeds, valid through
September 2027 for BMTC and August 2027 for the metro. Pass and fare rules were last
verified on 22 October 2026. Fares change by government notification, so always
check the official portal before paying.

### 40. Why does the agent sometimes refuse to quote a price?
Because some prices are not published in any accessible source. KSRTC corridor
season tickets and business-class fares are priced at the depot. It is better to
say "I cannot confirm this, here is the official link" than to invent a number you
would then budget around.
