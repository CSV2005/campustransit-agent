# Applying on Seva Sindhu — Step by Step, and What to Do When It Fails

## Before you start
Have these ready: Aadhaar number, a mobile number linked to Aadhaar for OTP,
the current-year fee receipt, an institution-signed pass form, a passport-size
photograph, and a caste certificate with RD number if you are an SC/ST applicant.
Decide which counter you will collect the pass from, because the form asks for it.

## The application, step by step
1. Open the Seva Sindhu / e-District portal at sevaone.karnataka.gov.in.
2. Register or log in. Aadhaar-based login with an OTP to the linked mobile is the
   usual route; a user account with password also works.
3. Choose "Apply for Services", then "View all available services", and search for
   the student bus pass service for the current academic year.
4. Pick the operator: BMTC for city travel, KSRTC for a mofussil corridor such as
   Kanakapura Road, or NWKRTC / KKRTC in other divisions.
5. Fill in the student's details, institution, course, year and the route between
   residence and institution.
6. Download the generated application form. Print it, get it signed and stamped by
   the institution, scan it, and upload it.
7. Upload the supporting documents. Keep each file under the portal's size limit
   and in the requested format.
8. Select the pass-issuing counter and submit.
9. Track the application with the acknowledgement number. On approval an SMS gives
   the counter and collection window.
10. Collect the smart card in person. A live photograph is taken at the counter.

## Timelines
Applications normally open in late May and passes are issued from 1 June, staggered
by category: school and PUC passes first, degree and professional courses from
July, technical and medical courses from August. A student who applies in October is
late but not excluded; the pass is still issued for the remainder of the academic
year.

## If the portal rejects the application
- **"Institution not found"** — the institution's DISE or AISHE code is missing from
  the dropdown. Ask the college office for the exact code and try the nearest
  matching entry, or apply at a Bangalore One counter.
- **"Aadhaar authentication failed"** — the mobile number linked to Aadhaar is stale.
  Update it at an Aadhaar centre, then retry. Do not create a second account.
- **"Document not clear"** — rescan at a higher resolution. Photographs taken at an
  angle or with a shadow are the usual cause.
- **"Category mismatch"** — the course category selected does not match the fee
  receipt. Correct the category, not the receipt.
- **"Counter unavailable"** — the chosen counter does not handle that category. Pick
  another from the list.

## If you already paid
Students who paid for a 2026-27 pass before the free scheme took effect should claim
a refund on Seva Sindhu: log in with the registered mobile and OTP, complete Aadhaar
e-KYC, and submit bank account details. KSRTC has said refunds are credited within
seven days of applying. Keep the old pass until the refund arrives; it stays valid
until then.

## Help and escalation
- Seva Sindhu helpline: 080-24410574. Email: sevasindhu@karnataka.gov.in.
- BMTC pass counters: Majestic (Kempegowda Bus Station), Kengeri, Shantinagar,
  Hosakote, Electronic City and Anekal are the commonly used Bengaluru counters.
- KSRTC publishes the list of its pass-issuing counters for each academic year;
  there were 123 counters for 2026-27.
- For a corridor-specific KSRTC season ticket, ask at the depot that operates the
  route, not at a BMTC counter.

## What to do if the pass has not arrived before term starts
Buy single tickets and keep the receipts. Most corporations reimburse pass-period
travel if the delay is caused by the corporation or the portal, but only with
receipts and a written complaint. Photograph every ticket or keep the UPI messages.
