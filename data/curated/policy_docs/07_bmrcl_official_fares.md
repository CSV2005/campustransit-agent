## Smart card and NCMC discounts on Namma Metro
Smart card and NCMC fares are discounted from the token fare:
- Monday to Saturday: 5% during peak hours and 10% during non-peak hours.
- Sundays and national holidays (26 January, 15 August, 2 October): 10% throughout the business day.
- Non-peak hours run from the start of revenue hours to 08:00, from 12:00 to 16:00, and from 21:00 to the end of revenue hours. All other times are peak.

## Children below 3 feet travel free
Tickets are not required for children below 3 feet. No age criteria is considered.

## Penalty for ticketless travel or a lost ticket
Penalty for ticketless travel or lost ticket is Rs.200/- apart from maximum token fare.

## Penalty for mismatch (skipped entry or exit)
Skipping of any entry or exit is termed as MISMATCH. The penalty for mismatch is Rs10.Minimum Fare will be charged for entering into a station.

## Token validity and revalidation
Entry through the Automatic Gate must be made within 30 minutes of purchasing a token. After 30 minutes, an administrative charge of Rs. 5/- applies for revalidation.

## Overstay rule and penalty
A passenger holding a valid ticket can exit from paid area of same station within 20 minutes and from paid area of other stations within 120 minutes. For overstaying in paid area beyond these limits, penalty of Rs 50 per hour is charged subject to a maximum of Rs 100.

## Luggage allowance
One Luggage, with a maximum dimension of 60 cm x 45 cm x 25 cm (length x breadth x height) is allowed per person. To allow commuters to carry extra luggage/ additional baggage and oversized baggages, charges at the rate of Rs. 30/- per baggage is levied. This luggage ticket can be purchased from Customer Care Center(CCC).

## Penalty for unpaid luggage
If ticket is not purchased for the additional baggage, then a penalty of Rs.250/- will be imposed. In case of any dispute, notwithstanding that commuter holds a valid ticket for travel, will be liable to be removed from the train by any metro railway officials authorised by the metro railway administration in this behalf or by any other person whom such metro railway official may call to his aid.

## Smart card validity extension
Add-value in the Smart Card shall be valid for ten years from the date of last add-value. The validity extension can be done by paying administrative charges of Rs. 20/- at Customer Care Center of any station.

## How many tokens one passenger may buy
The maximum number of tokens which are to be issued by the Ticket office operator to a single passenger at a time is six.

## Token removed from the exit gate
If the commuter takes away the token from the exit door without paying the deposit of Rs. 200.00 is penalized.

## How to top up a smart card
Stored Value can be added to Smart Card at any BMRCL Ticket counters or through online mode.through Namma Metro App(official) , BMRCL website or PayTM App, or Amazon Pay App).

## Smart card discount over the token fare
Get 5% discount for smart card, over token fare.

## Minimum top-up and cost of a new smart card
The top-up in case of Stored Value ticket shall be minimum Rs50 and further in multiples of Rs50. The cost of contactless smart card is Rs50 which is different from the stored value.

## Maximum stored value on a smart card
Total maximum stored value on a smart card is Rs 3000/-. when top-up done at BMRCL metro stations. However, when top up is done at other outlets i.e. through BMRCL website or Namma Metro App or PayTM or Amazon Pay the amount is restricted to Rs 2950/-

## Group booking discount (100 to 1000 people)
A discount of 20% over the token fare, where the commuters enter a station all together and exit from a common destination station all together

## Group booking discount (more than 1000 people)
A discount of 25% over the token fare, where the commuters enter a station all together and exit from a common destination station all together.
