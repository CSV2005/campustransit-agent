# BMTC and KSRTC Student Bus Passes — Basics

## Who issues what
BMTC issues student passes for travel inside Bengaluru's city network. KSRTC issues
student passes for travel on mofussil (inter-city / inter-town) services, including
the Kanakapura Road corridor that runs through Harohalli. Both corporations use the
same state application portal, Seva Sindhu, and the same broad category structure.

## Student pass categories and the pre-2026 fee structure
Pass fees depend on the course, not on the distance travelled. The published
2025-26 category fees were:

| Category | Pass fee (2025-26) | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| Primary school (classes 1-7) | Rs 150 | June 2025 - March 2026 |
| High school boys (classes 8-10) | Rs 750 | June 2025 - March 2026 |
| High school girls (classes 8-10) | Rs 550 | June 2025 - March 2026 |
| PUC (classes 11-12) | Rs 1,050 | June 2025 - March 2026 |
| PUC evening college | Rs 1,630 | June 2025 - March 2026 |
| Degree, professional, diploma (ITI) | Rs 1,300 | July 2025 - June 2026 |
| Technical and medical | Rs 1,830 | August 2025 - July 2026 |
| Evening college and PhD | Rs 1,630 | July 2025 - June 2026 |
| SC/ST students (all categories) | Rs 150 | As per category |

These published fees are now largely historical: from 12 June 2026 the state pays
the pass fee for all students on non-AC services (see the free pass document). The
figures remain useful because the *category* you fall into still decides the pass
validity window and the distance limits that apply.

## What a student pass actually covers
A student pass covers unlimited travel on the services named on the pass, for the
validity window, between the student's residence and the institution. It is not a
city-wide travel card:

- BMTC student passes cover BMTC services inside the city network, including
  non-AC ordinary buses and metro feeder services.
- KSRTC student passes cover KSRTC ordinary services on the declared route, under
  the existing distance norm of up to 60 km one-way between residence and
  institution.
- Neither pass covers AC Vajra, Vayu Vajra airport services, Airavat, Ambari,
  Rajahamsa or other luxury classes unless the pass is explicitly an AC pass.

## Documents required
The standard document set for a student pass application is:

1. The pass application form, downloaded from Seva Sindhu and signed and stamped by
   the institution.
2. A bonafide certificate from the institution, or the current academic year's fee
   receipt, which serves as proof of enrolment.
3. Aadhaar card.
4. A passport-size photograph. For a smart-card pass, a live photograph is captured
   at the counter when the card is printed.
5. A caste certificate with an RD number, for SC/ST applicants.
6. A mobile number registered on Seva Sindhu, for OTP and collection SMS.

Students who live more than 40 km from the institution may be asked for a
declaration that the course is not available at a closer institution. This affects
many Harohalli and Kanakapura Road commuters.

## Where to apply
Applications are submitted online at the Seva Sindhu / e-District portal
(sevaone.karnataka.gov.in). Applying online is free. Students who need help can use
a Bangalore One, Karnataka One or Grama One centre for a service charge of Rs 30.
After approval, an SMS tells the student which counter to collect the smart card
from. The student must collect it in person.

BMTC collection counters include Majestic (Kempegowda Bus Station), Kengeri,
Shantinagar, Hosakote, Electronic City and Anekal. KSRTC notifies its own list of
pass-issuing counters, which for 2026-27 ran to 123 counters across Karnataka.

## Renewal, replacement and route changes
Student passes are issued for an academic year and must be renewed each year
through the same Seva Sindhu process. If a pass card is lost or damaged, or if the
student changes route, BMTC charges a fixed replacement or route-change fee
(historically Rs 100). Renewal requires the same document set plus the previous
pass.

## Common reasons applications are rejected
- The application form is not signed and stamped by the institution.
- The fee receipt belongs to a previous academic year.
- The name or date of birth does not match the Aadhaar record.
- The photograph is not a recent passport-style photograph.
- The selected collection counter does not handle the applicant's category.
- For SC/ST applicants, the caste certificate RD number is missing.
