# Karnataka Free Student Bus Pass (2026-27)

## What changed
On 12 June 2026 the Karnataka Transport Department issued an order making bus
passes free for all students travelling on non-AC services of the four state road
transport corporations: BMTC, KSRTC, NWKRTC and KKRTC. The scheme was announced by
Chief Minister D.K. Shivakumar in the first cabinet meeting after taking office and
implemented within days.

The state bears the pass fee, a processing fee of Rs 100 per pass and an accident
relief fund contribution of Rs 50 per pass (Rs 5 per month of accident insurance
cover). The estimated annual cost of the scheme is Rs 286.08 crore, plus about
Rs 15 crore in processing and accident-relief charges.

## Who is eligible
- Any regular student enrolled in a recognised school, PU college, degree college,
  diploma, ITI, professional course, medical course, evening college, postgraduate
  or PhD programme in Karnataka.
- Karnataka-domicile students studying in institutions in neighbouring states.
- Students from districts bordering Karnataka who study at institutions in
  Karnataka.
- Existing eligibility rules still apply: academic requirements, distance limits
  (up to 60 km one-way between residence and institution under KSRTC norms) and
  pass validity periods are unchanged.

Girls and transgender students who are Karnataka residents already travel free
under the Shakti scheme and do not need a separate student pass, although they may
still apply if they prefer to hold a card.

## What the free pass does not cover
- BMTC AC services, including Vajra and Vayu Vajra airport buses.
- KSRTC luxury classes: Airavat, Airavat Club Class, Ambari, Ambari Dream Class,
  Rajahamsa, Rajahamsa Executive, Pallakki, Flybus and EV Power Plus.
- Travel on the portion of an interstate route that leaves Karnataka.
- Namma Metro. The metro is run by BMRCL, not by the four RTCs, and is outside this
  scheme.

A student who needs AC travel must still buy the paid BMTC AC student monthly pass,
which cost Rs 1,400 per month as of the January 2025 revision.

## How to apply
1. Go to the Seva Sindhu / e-District portal at sevaone.karnataka.gov.in and log in
   with Aadhaar or mobile OTP.
2. Search for the student bus pass service for the current academic year and choose
   the operator (BMTC, KSRTC, NWKRTC or KKRTC).
3. Fill in personal details, institution details, course and the residence-to-
   institution route.
4. Download the application form, get it signed and stamped by the institution, and
   upload it with the current-year fee receipt, photograph, Aadhaar and, for SC/ST
   applicants, the caste certificate with RD number.
5. Select a convenient pass-issuing counter.
6. On approval, an SMS gives the counter address. Collect the smart card in person;
   a live photograph is taken for the card.

Applying online is free. Applications submitted through Bangalore One, Karnataka
One or Grama One centres attract the usual Rs 30 service charge.

## Refunds for passes already bought
Students who had already paid for a 2026-27 pass between 29 May and 9 June 2026 -
19,792 passes were issued in that window - are entitled to a full refund. Refunds
are claimed on Seva Sindhu with Aadhaar-based e-KYC and bank account details, and
KSRTC has said the money is credited within seven days of applying. Students who
already hold a paid pass can continue to use it until the refund is processed.

## Practical implications for a Harohalli commuter
A student commuting from Jayanagar, Banashankari or Kanakapura Road to the DSU
Harohalli campus, which is roughly 35-45 km from most of Bengaluru, is exactly the
profile this scheme is aimed at. Before June 2026 the same trip needed either a
KSRTC student pass (Rs 1,050 for a college student, 10 months) or a BMTC pass plus
a KSRTC extension, plus daily tickets for the gaps. From 2026-27 the non-AC pass
costs the student nothing, so the only paid alternatives worth considering are the
AC passes and the metro.

## Verification advice
The scheme is recent and the rules are still settling. Students should confirm the
current syllabus of fees, counter list and eligibility on Seva Sindhu before paying
anything at a counter. Any agent that quotes an exact fee without checking the
portal is guessing.
